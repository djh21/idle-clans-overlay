Boosts I own (recomputes every row for your account)

Speed boosts (tool + cape + enchant + other) add together and cut the action time: 10 s at 25% becomes 7.5 s. Doubles raise the expected output (XP is for one item). Numbers from the Idle Clans wiki; check the in-game shop if yours differ.

Settings are remembered in this browser only. Profit = output × better of (bid after tax, NPC value) − inputs × ask − gold costs, at base action speed with no boosts. Inputs with no sell offer use the 24h average; if that is missing too the row is flagged.
